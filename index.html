How it works

Two quick guides. Pick a button at the top to start.

1

Red tag guide

For anything that should not be in the work area

  1. Find itLook for items that are unused, broken, in excess, of unknown owner or in the wrong place.
  2. Tag itFill in date, your name, SAP ID, department, area, item name, material code and quantity.
  3. Classify itChoose one action: relocate, repair, recycle, dispose, move to stores or storage bin.
  4. Add proofAttach a photo, add comments and the 5S area leader name, then log the red tag.
  5. Close it outDo the chosen action and remove the physical tag once the item is moved, repaired or disposed.
2

Disposal guide

For material that leaves the area as waste or scrap

  1. Name the sourceSelect where the material is generated and give the item name.
  2. Categorise itHazardous, non-hazardous, recyclable, metal scrap, used oil, chemical, e-waste and so on.
  3. Set frequency and quantityHow often it is generated and how much, with the unit (for example 200 kg per month).
  4. Define storageEnter the storage location and its capacity until the material is lifted.
  5. Define disposalWrite the method or procedure and who disposes of it (vendor, recycler, scrap yard).
  6. Register itThe entry is recorded and a weekly report is mailed to the HODs.
